Why the Gold Jar Keeps Coming Back in Korea

In Korea, IOPE Super Vital Cream is one of those creams people buy twice: once for themselves, and once as a gift. The gold jar has been IOPE's flagship anti-aging cream for years, and the NEW 6th generation is the newest refresh of the line.

IOPE says the 6th generation brings together 18 years of Super Vital know-how and introduces Senomune™, a next-generation aging-care ingredient. The brand also claims skin-aging indicators improve after a single use. We kept that in mind and focused on what you actually feel day to day.

Texture: Rich, but Not Heavy

The cream is thick when you scoop it, but it absorbs quickly and doesn't feel sticky. We used it as the last step at night. In the morning, skin still felt moist, and foundation went on smoothly over it.

After regular use, skin looked smoother and flaked less. Korean users often add a thin second layer on the eye area and smile lines, and that works well. The scent is soft and fades quickly.
